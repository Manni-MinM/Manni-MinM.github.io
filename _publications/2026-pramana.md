Networking research advances by turning hypotheses into empirical evidence. Pramana is a composable, domain-specific backend that reduces the lag between ideation and data generation. For example, it can configure a realistic bottleneck link, generate competing application traffic (e.g. a bulk BBR download alongside Google Meet), and collect service-quality metrics, without starting from scratch for each new idea.

To appear at ACM HotNets 2026. Preprint: [arXiv:2607.26352](https://arxiv.org/abs/2607.26352)
